The dataset used in the study appears to be too small to support robust conclusions.
The references are largely outdated; many are preprints or conference papers, and several do not follow proper APA formatting.
The authors primarily compare their approach with pre-trained models rather than with relevant existing studies in the literature.

The manuscript does not clearly highlight its major contributions.
