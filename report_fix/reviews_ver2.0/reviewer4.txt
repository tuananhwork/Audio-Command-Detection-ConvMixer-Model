Reviewer 4

Technical Questions
1)Model Design Justification
What motivated the choice of the ConvMixer configuration (256 channels, 8 layers), and was any hyperparameter tuning performed to optimize this setup?
2)Dataset Size and Generalization
Given the relatively small dataset (1,853 samples), how does the model avoid overfitting, and how well would it generalize to larger or more diverse datasets?
3)Preprocessing Impact
How much does each preprocessing step (denoising, WebRTC VAD, normalization) individually contribute to the final performance?
4) Comparison Fairness
Can you elaborate on how fairness was ensured when comparing ConvMixer with architectures like ResNet-18, MobileNetV2, EfficientNet, and AST?
5)Computational Efficiency
What are the inference latency and memory usage of ConvMixer compared to the other models, especially for edge deployment scenarios?
6)Ablation Study
Was an ablation study conducted to evaluate the importance of patch-based design versus standard convolutional approaches?

Non-Technical / Broader Impact Questions
1)Practical Applications
What real-world applications (e.g., voice assistants, IoT devices) would benefit most from this ConvMixer-based approach?
2)Language Generalization
Since the dataset is Vietnamese, how well would the model perform on other languages or multilingual datasets?
3)Accessibility and Deployment
How feasible is it to deploy this model on low-power or embedded devices in real-world environments?
4)Research Contribution and Novelty
What distinguishes this work from existing audio classification approaches, and what is the key novelty in adapting ConvMixer to the audio domain?
